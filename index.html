    Background & approach

    I'm a software engineer working across web development, cloud architecture, and databases. Day to day, that means building and maintaining systems on Azure, wiring up identity and DevOps pipelines, and making sure the data layer holds up under real usage.

    I started out studying cybersecurity at Bina Nusantara University. That background still shapes how I think about systems. I always ask what breaks and why. But building things won out over breaking them, and web programming became where I actually wanted to spend my time.

    These days I also spend a fair amount of time on AI tooling and prompt engineering, both for my own work and for workflows my teammates rely on. I'd rather ship something useful fast and improve it than sit on a plan too long.
